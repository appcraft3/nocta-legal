# Nocta – legal pages

Privacy policy, terms of use and support page for the Nocta – Romance Stories iOS app, served with GitHub Pages:

- https://appcraft3.github.io/nocta-legal/ (support)
- https://appcraft3.github.io/nocta-legal/privacy.html
- https://appcraft3.github.io/nocta-legal/terms.html

The app links to these URLs (`App/LegalLinks.swift` in the app repo) and App Store Connect lists them. Keep the privacy
policy in step with the app's `PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` and the App Store privacy answers.
