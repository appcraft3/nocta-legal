Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Nocta – Romance Stories app ("Nocta", "the app") handles and why. Nocta is published by ARB TECHNOLOGY AND TRADE CONSULTING OÜ (Jaama tn 101, 50604 Tartu, Estonia; "we", "us"), which is the data controller. Contact: rcanbabaoglu@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You don't create an account with a name, e-mail or phone number. The app makes an anonymous account in the background.
  • We store your game progress so it isn't lost, and we measure how the app is used so we can improve it.
  • We don't show ads in the app and we don't sell your data.
  • We advertise Nocta on other platforms. If you allow tracking when iOS asks, the app shares limited data with TikTok, including the advertising identifier (IDFA), to measure those ads. If you don't, TikTok receives nothing from the app.
  • You can delete your cloud save and account from inside the app: My Room → Account → Delete my data.

What we collect

  • Anonymous account ID. A random identifier created by Google Firebase Authentication when you first open the app. It is not linked to your name, e-mail or Apple ID.
  • Game data (cloud save). Your profile name and avatar choice, the names you give heroines, story progress and choices, endings, collected items, diamond and tea balances and what you have unlocked. Stored in Google Cloud Firestore so your progress survives a reinstall.
  • Usage data. Events such as chapters started and finished, choices bought and screens opened, plus device model, operating system, app version, language, an app-instance identifier and approximate location (country or city, derived from the IP address). Collected with Google Analytics for Firebase without the advertising identifier.
  • Purchases. Payments are processed by Apple; we never see your card or payment details. Our purchase provider RevenueCat receives your anonymous account ID, purchase and subscription records and basic device information to validate purchases and keep your subscription active across reinstalls.
  • Advertising measurement. The app includes TikTok's SDK, which runs only if you allow tracking (see below). It then sends TikTok app events (install, app open, returning after two days, first chapter finished, and purchases with product, price and currency) together with device data (identifier for vendors (IDFV), device model and IP address), so we can see how our ads perform. RevenueCat also receives an Apple Ads attribution token and our analytics app-instance identifier. Apple's SKAdNetwork and AdAttributionKit report anonymously to ad networks how far a new install has got: story started, first chapter finished, Pass bought, and a revenue band.

We do not collect your contacts, photos, precise location, health data or messages, and the app has no chat with other people.

Tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency)

When you finish setting up Nocta after a new install, iOS asks whether Nocta may track you. If you allow it, the TikTok SDK also sends your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) with the events above, and RevenueCat stores the IDFA, IDFV and IP address, so we can tell which of our ads led to an install or a purchase.

If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", or iOS has not asked you, the TikTok SDK doesn't run: no advertising identifier is collected and TikTok receives nothing from the app. We use this data only to measure and improve our own advertising. You can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Why we use it (legal bases)

  • To provide the game, save your progress and deliver what you buy (performance of a contract).
  • To understand which stories and features work, fix problems and keep the service secure (our legitimate interests).
  • To measure and improve our own advertising (your permission for TikTok and the advertising identifier; our legitimate interests for the Apple Ads token and Apple's anonymous conversion reports).
  • To meet legal obligations, for example tax and accounting records for purchases.

Who processes it for us

  • Google LLC / Google Ireland – Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Google Analytics for Firebase (Firebase privacy).
  • RevenueCat, Inc. – in-app purchase and subscription management, and advertising attribution (RevenueCat privacy).
  • TikTok – advertising measurement, under TikTok's own business terms (TikTok privacy).
  • Apple – App Store payments, SKAdNetwork and AdAttributionKit, under Apple's own privacy policy.

Some of these providers process data in the United States. Transfers are covered by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent safeguards offered by the providers.

How long we keep it

Game data and purchase records are kept while your anonymous account exists. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months. Purchase records may be kept longer where accounting law requires it. Data sent to TikTok is kept under TikTok's own terms.

Your rights and choices

  • Delete: My Room → Account → Delete my data deletes your cloud save and anonymous account and resets the app. You can also e-mail us with the account number shown in My Room. Deleting does not reach data TikTok has already received; see TikTok's privacy policy for your rights there.
  • Tracking: change or withdraw your permission at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Under the GDPR (and Turkish KVKK) you may ask for access to, correction, deletion or a copy of your data, and object to or restrict its use. Write to rcanbabaoglu@gmail.com.
  • You may complain to a data protection authority, for example the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (aki.ee) or, in Türkiye, the KVKK.

Age

Nocta contains romantic content for adults and is intended only for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18; if you believe a minor has used the app, contact us and we will delete the data.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.

Apple'ın SKAdNetwork ve AdAttributionKit servisleri, yeni bir kurulumun ne kadar ilerlediğini (hikâyeye başlama, ilk bölümü bitirme, Pass satın alma ve gelir aralığı) reklam ağlarına anonim olarak bildirir.

Takip izni (App Tracking Transparency)

Yeni bir kurulumdan sonra Nocta'yı ayarlamayı bitirdiğinde iOS, Nocta'nın seni takip etmesine izin verip vermediğini sorar. İzin verirsen TikTok SDK'sı yukarıdaki olaylarla birlikte cihazının reklam kimliğini (IDFA) de gönderir, RevenueCat de IDFA, IDFV ve IP adresini saklar; böylece hangi reklamımızın kuruluma ya da satın almaya yol açtığını görebiliriz.

"Uygulamadan takip etmemesini iste"yi seçersen ya da iOS sana sormadıysa TikTok SDK'sı çalışmaz: reklam kimliği toplanmaz ve TikTok uygulamadan hiçbir veri almaz. Bu verileri yalnızca kendi reklamlarımızı ölçmek ve geliştirmek için kullanırız. Seçimini istediğin zaman iOS Ayarlar › Gizlilik ve Güvenlik › Takip bölümünden değiştirebilirsin.
